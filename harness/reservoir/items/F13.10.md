# Assemble evidence pack and obtain Mitsui verifier sign-off

Collect the evidence for every acceptance criterion in item 9.4, obtain a Mitsui verifier sign-off or a written rejection reason for each, and send the pack as Deliverable 9, the evaluation report.

## User story

As the pilot lead, I want one evidence pack against the acceptance matrix so that Mitsui can sign off or reject each criterion.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When the evidence pack is assembled, the system shall link each acceptance criterion in item 9.4 to its evidence.
2. When a Mitsui verifier reviews a criterion, the pack shall record either a sign-off or a written rejection reason.
3. When Friday Dec 18 is reached, the pack shall have been sent with all evidence except the load evidence.
4. When the load evidence is available on Dec 24 and in the first week after the holiday, the system shall add it to the pack and send the update.
