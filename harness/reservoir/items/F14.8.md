Submit deliverables D10 and D11 with acceptance forms and record the 8-business-day review period.

2. When the submission is made, the system shall record the submission date and the 8-business-day review period.
3. The recorded review period shall end by Fri Jan 8, 2027.
4. When the acceptance owner signs, the system shall record the signed forms (DEP-23).
