# IT/PMO lead sign-off on the recorded sessions

Collect the IT/PMO lead's sign-off on the recorded sessions and test results as the feature's completion gate.

## User story

As the IT/PMO lead, I want to review the recorded sessions and sign off so that the feature is accepted by its owner.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When the recorded sessions from Claude Code and Claude Desktop are submitted, the system shall have a sign-off from the IT/PMO lead on file.
2. Where the access-control and write-refusal results exist, the sign-off shall reference them.
