# Switch Identity Center to Entra with SAML and SCIM

Make Entra ID the identity source for IAM Identity Center with SCIM, and keep permission sets and assignments as reviewable code.

## User story

As Soma (solution architect), I want IAM Identity Center to use Entra ID as its identity source with SCIM provisioning, so that Mitsui users sign in with their Mitsui accounts and Lake Formation can grant access by Entra group.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When the identity-source switch is done in the management account, IAM Identity Center shall use Entra ID as its identity source with SCIM provisioning.
2. When the stack is deployed, the system shall have the permission sets and group-to-account assignments defined as code.
3. When the switch is complete, each CapNexus engineer shall be able to sign in to the portal with an Entra account.
4. While a CapNexus engineer has no working Entra guest account, the switch shall not be performed.
