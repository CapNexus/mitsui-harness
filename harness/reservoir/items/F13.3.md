# Write-refusal test through AgentCore Policy and Lake Formation

Prove that every write attempt is refused by AgentCore Policy and, with Policy bypassed in a test, by Lake Formation.

## User story

As a security reviewer, I want write attempts shown to be refused at two layers so that the assistant cannot change data.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When a write attempt is made through the assistant, AgentCore Policy shall refuse it.
2. When AgentCore Policy is bypassed in a test and a write attempt is made, Lake Formation shall refuse it.
3. When the tests finish, the system shall save the log of every write attempt and its refusal.
