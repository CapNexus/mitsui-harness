Present the readout to Mitsui and record Noah Jackson's decision on the POC resources.

## User story

As Noah Jackson, I want to decide at the readout whether to keep, pause or tear down the resources, so that the POC ends in a clear state.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When the readout is held, the system shall record the date and attendees.
2. When the readout ends, the system shall record Noah Jackson's decision as exactly one of keep, pause or tear down.
3. If the decision is tear down, the system shall record that teardown follows the runbook procedure.
4. The recorded decision shall be stored with the handover documents.
