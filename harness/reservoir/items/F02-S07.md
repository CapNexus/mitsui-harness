# Create the POC workload account and enable IAM Identity Center

Create the POC account through Account Factory and enable Identity Center so POC resources have a governed home and CapNexus can work without the temporary role.

## User story

As Venkat (DevOps), I want the POC workload account created through Account Factory and IAM Identity Center enabled, so that all POC resources have a governed home and CapNexus can work without the temporary cross-account role.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When Account Factory creates the POC account from account_email_poc, the account shall be enrolled in the Workloads OU with no drift.
2. When Identity Center is enabled, it shall be the organization instance in us-east-1 with the identity source left as the default directory.
3. When the setup is complete, CapNexus engineers shall have temporary Identity Center users that reach the accounts after the cross-account role is deleted on Fri Nov 20.
