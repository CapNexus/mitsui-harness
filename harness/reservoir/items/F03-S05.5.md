Confirm and log that InfoSec has the architecture summary (DEP-05) and that any CAB ticket is approved before traffic is allowed.

## User story

As Venkat (DevOps), I want the review and change-control status recorded, so that traffic does not open before InfoSec and CAB are satisfied.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When traffic is about to open, the system shall record the architecture review status and any CAB ticket in the RAID log.
2. When Mike confirms in the RAID log that InfoSec has the architecture summary and that the CAB ticket (if needed) is approved, the system shall treat the gate as passed.
3. If the InfoSec confirmation or a required CAB approval is missing, the system shall keep traffic closed.
