# Run the Dec 14 to Dec 24 evidence export on Dec 24

On Thu Dec 24, run the export script for Dec 14 to Dec 24 and the freeze check, and save the outputs as the evidence for the unattended window.

## User story

As Noah Jackson (Mitsui), I want the exported history for the final two weeks, so that I can see the daily load ran without intervention.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When the export runs on Thu Dec 24 with --from 2026-12-14 --to 2026-12-24, the system shall save the DMS task history, Step Functions executions, load_runs and alarm history for that range under evidence/F04/unattended-window/.
2. If any execution in Dec 14 to Dec 24 was started manually, the export shall fail and the evidence shall not be marked complete.
3. When the export completes, the system shall save the output of scripts/check-load-freeze.sh --since 2026-12-14 next to the export.
