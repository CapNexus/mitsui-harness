# Log slipped dependencies and shift plan dates

Detect overdue dependencies, record slips with cause and days late, and shift plan dates day for day to document SOW Assumption 8.

## User story

As Mike (CapNexus project manager), I want every slipped Week 1 dependency logged with its date and cause and the plan shifted day for day, so that the SOW schedule protection (Assumption 8) is documented when a Mitsui dependency is late.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When a dependency's needed-by date has passed without status resolved, scripts/check-overdue.sh shall list it as overdue, and with --today 2026-11-17 shall exit non-zero and list DEP-01 and any other unresolved item due on or before that date.
2. If a dependency is marked slipped and its slip start date, cause or days late is empty, then scripts/check-dependency-register.sh shall fail.
3. When the shift helper (shift-plan.sh) runs for a slipped dependency, the helper shall shift plan dates by the number of days late, with a dry-run output available as evidence.
