# Cross-database join rules through the MEPWELL crosswalk

Give the assistant the rules and examples it needs to join MEPWELL, SDEDBPROD and MEPS_ARIES on well identifiers through the crosswalk.

## User story

As a well data analyst, I want questions that span databases answered correctly, so that I do not have to map well identifiers myself.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When a question needs data from more than one of MEPWELL, SDEDBPROD and MEPS_ARIES, the system shall join them on well identifiers through the MEPWELL crosswalk.
2. When the crosswalk has no match for a well identifier, the system shall say so in the answer instead of guessing a match.
3. Where an example question is provided, the system shall use its join pattern for similar questions.
