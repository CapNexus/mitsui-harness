# Send a test budget notification and get the billing contact's confirmation

Confirm the deployed AWS Budgets alert reaches the billing contact.

## User story

As Venkat (DevOps), I want the budget alert tested end to end, so that I know the billing contact will be notified of overspend.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When a test notification is sent from the deployed budget, the system shall deliver it to the billing contact.
2. When the billing contact confirms receipt, the system shall have the screenshot or reply saved in evidence/F02/pipeline/.

## Scope

- (not grounded)

- F02-S09.2: Deploy the baseline stacks through the pipeline and confirm a second run is a no-op

- Human gate: the billing contact confirms receipt of the test alert.
- The billing contact email is personal data of a Mitsui employee; keep it in parameters, not in committed evidence if avoidable.
