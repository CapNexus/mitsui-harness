# Get a Mitsui recipient to confirm the alarm email arrived

Complete the human gate for the fault test: a Mitsui recipient on the real distribution list confirms receipt, and the email is saved as evidence.

## User story

As a Mitsui DBA, I want to confirm the alarm email reaches our list, so that we know our team would be notified.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When the partial-load alarm email has been sent to the real alarm_distribution_list, the system shall record a confirmation of receipt from a Mitsui recipient.
2. When receipt is confirmed, the system shall have the received email saved under `evidence/F04/fault-test/`.
3. If the distribution list is still the CapNexus on-call placeholder, the system shall not mark the gate as passed.
