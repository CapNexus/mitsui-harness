# Remove CapNexus access after handover

Remove all CapNexus access to the Mitsui environment once handover and the decision are complete.

## User story

As the Mitsui IT operations owner, I want CapNexus access removed after handover, so that only Mitsui controls the environment.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When the knowledge-transfer session and the keep, pause or tear down decision are recorded, the system shall remove every CapNexus user, role and credential for the Mitsui environment.
2. When access is removed, the system shall record what was removed and when.
3. If CapNexus access remains after removal is attempted, the system shall report the remaining access to the Mitsui IT operations owner.

## Scope

- (not grounded)

## Depends on

- F14.2: Hand over code, IaC and configuration in the agreed repository
- F14.3: Hold the knowledge-transfer session
- F14.6: Hold the readout and record the keep, pause or tear down decision

## Ship notes

- Access changes are hard to reverse; confirm the target accounts before removal.
- Must happen after the decision, since teardown may need CapNexus access.
