# Create the shared Teams channel and confirm CapNexus access

Get guest invitations and Teams access working for the five CapNexus engineers so they can work with Mitsui staff.

## User story

As a CapNexus engineer, I want to sign in to the shared Teams channel, the AWS access portal and Entra as a guest, so that I can work with Mitsui staff and reach the POC environment.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When Mitsui sends invitations from the guest invitation list, each of the five CapNexus engineers shall receive one and confirm receipt by reply.
2. When an engineer opens the shared Teams channel, the engineer shall be able to post a message, with a screenshot of the test post saved in user-stories/evidence/F01/.
3. If a CapNexus engineer still lacks access when the due date passes, then the RAID log shall record a slipped DEP-29 with the date.
