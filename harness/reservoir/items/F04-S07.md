# Add load alarms and the email topic

Add CloudWatch alarms and an encrypted SNS email topic so a failed, partial or skipped daily load is reported the same day.

## User story

As a Mitsui DBA on the distribution list, I want an email when the daily load fails, is partial, or skips SDEDBPROD, so that someone acts the same day.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When the CDK app is synthesized, it shall contain alarms for DMS task failed or stopped, partial load, missed load (no LoadSucceeded data point within 2 hours after the window end) and SDEDBPROD skipped, each with the SNS topic as its alarm action.
2. The SNS topic shall be encrypted with the customer-managed KMS key.
3. The SNS topic shall have an email subscription to alarm_distribution_list.
