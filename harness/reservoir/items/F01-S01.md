Add the RAID log and a register checker, and seed both, so every dependency and risk has an owner and a date.

## User story

As Mike (CapNexus project manager), I want one tracker that holds every client dependency and every risk, assumption, issue and decision, so that nobody has to ask who owns an item or when it is due.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

1. When scripts/check-dependency-register.sh runs, the script shall exit 0 only if DEP-01 to DEP-29 all exist in user-stories/dependencies/README.md and each has a non-empty owner, needed-by date, type and status.
2. The RAID log at user-stories/raid-log.md shall have sections for Risks, Assumptions, Issues and Decisions.
3. The RAID log shall be seeded with Feature List Section 5 (gaps 1 to 9) as issues and Section 7 (questions 1 to 7) as open decisions, each with an owner and a date.
